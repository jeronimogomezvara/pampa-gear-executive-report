# One-page executive report in Google Sheets

**Tools:** Google Sheets

## What I did

I built a one-page executive report in Google Sheets from more than 4,000 sales rows of an online store (2024 to 2025).

- **Four KPIs:** total revenue, orders, average ticket and growth of 2025 vs 2024.
- **Three charts** with titles that state the conclusion: monthly revenue trend, revenue by category and revenue share by customer segment.
- **Pivot tables** for every calculation, kept in a separate area so the report shows only what a manager needs to read.
- **A segment filter** that updates every KPI and chart at once.

## Findings

REVENUE GREW 43.72% in 2025.

- Revenue peaks every August to October. 

- Training and Footwear drives 58% of the total revenue.

- Clubs generate 65% of revenue from just 44% of orders.

## What I learned

Every chart title states the conclusion, not the topic. A manager should get the point in ten seconds.
